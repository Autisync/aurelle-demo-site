How a treatment works here

The same three steps, whether it's your first peel or your tenth anti-wrinkle appointment.

  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes with Claire or Maya. We look at your skin, ask about your health and medication, and agree a plan. It's free, and redeemable against treatment.

  2. Treatment

    On the day or another day, never rushed. Prescription treatments are only ever given by Claire, a registered nurse prescriber.

  3. Review at two weeks

    We see you again to check the result and adjust if needed, at no charge. We'll remind you when your next one is due.
